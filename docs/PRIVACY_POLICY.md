[YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we") publishes the mobile game **Lumo**. This policy explains what information is processed when you play.

## Information we process

- **Game progress** (scores, coins, skins, settings) is stored **only on your device**. We don't have servers that receive it.
- **Advertising.** Lumo shows ads from **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to approximate location), and ad-interaction and diagnostic data, in order to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**. On iOS, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking. See [Google's policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Purchases.** In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We use **RevenueCat** to validate purchases. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. See [RevenueCat's policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

We do not sell your personal information. Lumo is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices

- Reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored game data.
- For access or deletion requests related to our partners, contact us and we will help.
